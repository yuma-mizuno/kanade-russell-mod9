# Three Kanade–Russell Identities Modulo Nine

This paper proves three of the Kanade–Russell identities modulo nine. See:

[[2609.09653] The three Kanade-Russell identities modulo nine](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09653)

For the Lean 4 formalization, see:

[yuma-mizuno/KR-mod9-lean: Lean 4 formalization of the three Kanade-Russell identities modulo nine](https://github.com/yuma-mizuno/KR-mod9-lean)

The day after the initial release of this manuscript on this repository, the following preprint, which independently proves five of the modulo 9 Kanade–Russell identities, appeared on arXiv:

[[2609.08816] The modulo 9 Kanade--Russell identities and their Nahm-sum duals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08816)

This repository is retained as a timestamped record of the work.
